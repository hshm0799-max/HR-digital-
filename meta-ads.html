Meta Ads

Meta Ads Basics: Reach, Engage, Convert

How Facebook and Instagram ads are structured, which terms matter and which numbers to track. HR Digital builds and manages Meta Ads for businesses across India.

CampaignObjective: the result you want, such as leads, sales or messages.
→
Ad SetAudience, budget, placements and schedule.
→
AdThe creative, copy and call-to-action people see.

Building blocks

Campaign
The top level where you choose the main advertising objective.
Ad Set
Controls the audience, budget, placements and schedule.
Ad
The creative that people actually see.
Objective
The business result you want from the campaign.
Audience
The people you want to reach.
Budget
The amount you plan to spend.
Placement
Where your ad appears, such as Feed, Stories or Reels.
Creative
The image, video, copy and button used in the ad.

Key metrics

CPM
Cost per 1,000 impressions.
CPC
Cost per link click.
CTR
Share of people who click after seeing the ad.
CPL
Cost per lead.
CPA
Cost per acquisition or action.
ROAS
Revenue generated divided by ad spend.
Frequency
Average number of times one person sees your ad.
Impressions
Total number of times the ad is shown.
Reach
Number of unique people who saw the ad.

Tracking and targeting

Meta Pixel
A website tracking tool that measures actions after a click.
Conversions API
Sends website events to Meta from your server for more reliable measurement.
Retargeting
Showing ads to people who already interacted with your business.
Lookalike Audience
People similar to a source audience, such as your existing customers.

Meta Ads Questions

A campaign sets the objective, an ad set sets the audience, budget, placements and schedule, and an ad is the creative with copy and a call-to-action that people see.

It depends on the goal. For leads, watch CPL and CTR. For sales, watch CPA and ROAS. CPM, reach and frequency show how efficiently the ad is delivered.

They are strongly recommended. The Pixel and Conversions API let Meta measure actions on your website, which helps with optimisation, retargeting and lookalike audiences.
